  4. Choose Templates → PulseVFX and drag a look onto a clip.
  5. With the clip selected, adjust it in the Inspector — every control is native and keyframeable.

DaVinci Resolve Free 21.1.1 — macOS Live Bridge Setup

How this tool connects to Resolve Free 21.1.1 using the Lua File-Mailbox + Fusion.prefs channel

DaVinci Resolve Free 21.1 / 21.1.1 Technical Architecture:

In Resolve 19.1, Blackmagic restricted UIManager to Studio. In Resolve 21.1 / 21.1.1, Blackmagic disabled Python (.py) scripts in the Free edition and sandboxed Lua (disabling io.open, os.execute, and require).

How Our Bridge Works in Resolve Free 21.1.1: Resolve Free 21.1.1 still runs .lua scripts from Workspace ▸ Scripts with access to resolve, MediaPool, Timeline, Fusion, loadfile(), and fusion:SavePrefs(). Our resident bridge script (VFXKit_ResolveFree_Bridge.lua) reads requests via loadfile() and responds through Fusion.prefs in ~50ms.

Install the Lua Bridge & Native Fusion Macros (macOS)

Copies VFXKit_ResolveFree_Bridge.lua into ~/Library/Application Support/Blackmagic Design/DaVinci Resolve/Fusion/Scripts/Utility/ and installs our zero-plugin .setting macros.
